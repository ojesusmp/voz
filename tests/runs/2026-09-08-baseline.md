# Baseline run, 2026-09-08, before the parity work

The artifact behind the number in `tests/README.md`. Kept because a baseline with no evidence
behind it is exactly the weasel attribution the skill tells you not to write.

**Method.** A fresh agent was given only the four pre-parity voz files (`SKILL.md`,
`references/ai-tells.md`, `references/human-prose.md`, `references/self-check.md`) from a
pristine backup, plus `fixtures/slop-draft.md`, and asked to audit without rewriting. It had no
access to `no-ai-slop`, no answer key, and no knowledge that a comparison was coming.

**Result: 22 distinct problems found**, out of 32 planted.

Caught: emoji heading, Title Case heading, throat-clearing opener, "when it comes to", the binary
contrast, "at the end of the day", delve, leverage, robust, seamless, ever-evolving, rule of
three (two instances), weasel attribution (both), the inflated copula, "pivotal", the
significance tail, the unearned vague claim, negative listing, mechanical boldface, the
"In conclusion" template, multifaceted, underscores, and the kicker (misfiled as another negative
parallelism rather than named as a kicker).

Missed, all planted deliberately:
faux-insight setup, rhetorical setup, colon reveal, interpretive metadiscourse, synonym cycling,
dramatic fragmentation, every empty adverb (just, actually, fundamentally, truly, literally),
four empty phrases ("the truth is", "in order to", "going forward", "let's dive in"), both
nominalizations, the abstraction doing a human verb, sentence case after a colon, both
portability failures, and the word "streamline".

**Two structural findings, in the agent's own words, unprompted.**

On editing a draft written by someone else: "No, not really. The four files are written entirely
as instructions for the act of writing/drafting."

On an audit mode: "No, it explicitly disclaims this." It then observed that what it had just
done, listing problems without rewriting, "isn't a mode the guidance itself defines or endorses."

**The most revealing miss.** It listed "That's it. That's the whole thing." under things to LEAVE
ALONE, praising it as good short rhythm. That exact string is a named cliche. Pre-parity voz not
only failed to catch it, its unbounded "fragments are allowed" rule actively defended it. The
bound now lives in `human-prose.md` and `ai-tells.md`.

**After the parity work, same draft, same method: 32 of 32 found, 6 of 6 preservation traps
intact.** On `fresh-draft.md`, which shares no wording with voz: 19 of 19 valid classes, 5 of 5
traps intact, nine of them identified from the pattern description rather than a printed phrase.
